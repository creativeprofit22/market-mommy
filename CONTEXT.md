# Shared vocabulary

- **Business stage** — The help a person currently needs: finding a direction, getting a first customer, or, later, making sales consistent. It is correctable and is not a payment tier or automatic reward for a sale.
- **Payment plan** — Commercial access or usage terms for this product. It does not describe the user's business progress; no pricing or subscription is selected.
- **Observation** — What a source actually says or what someone records happening, without an inferred explanation. A buyer's stated intent is not a received payment.
- **Evidence** — An observation with traceable origin, timing, permitted use, and limitations that can support or contradict a claim. Reposts do not create independent corroboration.
- **Interpretation** — A contextual explanation of what evidence could mean. It remains separate from the observation and may change when a profile or source is corrected.
- **Recommendation** — A versioned, evidence-linked next action fitted to the person's constraints, with assumptions, unknowns, and reasons to stop or reconsider. It is not an earnings forecast.
- **Experiment** — A bounded real-world test whose original assumptions, intended actions, and success/stop conditions are recorded before outcomes. Imagined buyer reactions are not experiment outcomes.
- **Paid commitment** — An agreement to buy for an explicit amount and currency, whether or not money has arrived. It is not revenue received.
- **Payment received** — Money recorded as actually received, with amount, currency, date, and basis of the record. Refunds and costs remain separate; receipt alone does not establish profit.
