# Evidence policy

Status: future design requirements, not implemented collection, source permission or legal certification. [Contracts](contracts.md) owns records/statuses; [evaluation](evaluation.md) owns quantitative acceptance; [operations](operations.md) owns enforcement.

## Provenance and claim support

Keep observation, evidence, interpretation and recommendation separate. Every usable evidence item retains origin identity and URL when relevant, a supporting excerpt or permitted snapshot reference, collection time, known publication/observation times, applicable segment, method/version and rights/retention context. Unknown dates stay unknown. A URL alone does not show that the source supports the claim; retain enough permitted context for review.

Recommendations cite supporting and contradicting evidence versions and identify assumptions. Citation existence, source credibility, freshness and claim support are different checks. Paid commitments, received payments, costs and delivery hours are separate first-party observations. Self-report is not independent verification. Funding, hiring, complaints, likes and search volume are proxies, not proven willingness to buy.

## Independence and relevance

Deduplicate identical and near-identical observations, reposts and syndication under a common origin. Keep provenance links rather than multiplying corroboration. Distinguish firsthand experience, marketing, rumor and interpretation. Check segment and geography before transferring a claim to this audience. Do not infer population sentiment or demand prevalence from a convenience sample. Agent agreement and synthetic customers are not independent market evidence.

## Freshness and coverage

A source-specific policy must state which claims expire, review triggers and permitted refresh/retention. Exact periods remain open until sources and decision use are selected; no universal freshness interval is invented here. Until configured, freshness is unknown and cannot support an unqualified current claim. Collection today does not make an old statement current.

Track the requested versus checked scope and partial/unvisited portions. Successful zero-result collection, missing coverage, stale evidence, unknown dates, access rejection and provider failure are distinct. A time-sensitive recommendation either uses appropriately checked current evidence or abstains/explicitly limits its claim. Preserve contrary evidence rather than selecting only favorable examples.

## Permitted-source gate

Start with non-sensitive synthetic fixtures or authorized manual observations. An external adapter requires documented source permission/terms, collection method, intended use, data fields, provider processing/retention and cost authorization before activation. Prefer official/authorized interfaces; a public page, robots setting or vendor capability alone does not establish reuse rights. Do not bypass authentication or access restrictions.

Collect only necessary business information. Exclude sensitive-trait inference, covert tracking, personal-life profiling, leaked data and speculative allegations. Avoid unnecessary customer identities/contact details in pilot records. US scope does not remove privacy, professional-person data, outreach or state-law questions. Source selection and real-user launch require relevant security/privacy/legal review; this document is not that review.

## Corrections and dependent claims

Record a correction as a versioned supersession with reason and provenance. Mark dependent interpretations/recommendations for reassessment immediately; do not keep presenting them as current. Preserve prior reasoning and original experiment conditions when lawful so changes are explainable. A new profile can change interpretation without changing the observation.

Deletion/removal must honor permitted retention and user rights: remove restricted content from active records, caches, exports and recovery handling as applicable; retain only a lawful minimal tombstone/dependency marker, never an assumption that auditability permits keeping forbidden text. Backups need a documented correction/deletion policy before real data. Reassess claims whose support disappears. This phase performs no deletion.

## Untrusted content boundary

Source text, imports and model/tool output are data, never authorization. An embedded request to change policy, reveal credentials, run commands or contact a destination must not become an action. Normalize and validate imported records and output schemas; source strings cannot select arbitrary filesystem paths, network destinations or tools. Human review complements, but does not replace, deterministic permission and spending gates.
